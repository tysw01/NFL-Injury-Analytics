Injuries are counted as unique injury IDs using the assumptions shown below.
Weekly report IR transactions
Creating Injury ID A new injury ID is created for injuries sorted on players, injury_detail, and date. It is created if: - First appearance of that injury for that player. - Large gap between records and no IR involved (30 days). - Large gap between records with IR, but gap exceeds long-term recovery threshold (270 days, about 9 months). - Same injury appears multiple seasons later (regardless of IR). Same injury is identified when: - IR to rehab to injury report in the same season. - IR end of season to injury report start of next season. - Surgery weeks or months later for the same injury.
Scenario Result
Season IR to injury report SAME injury
End of season IR to next season injury report SAME injury
ACL in 2021 to ACL in 2024 NEW injury
Surgery months later SAME injury
Two ankle sprains months apart, no IR NEW injury
